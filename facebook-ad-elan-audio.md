## Targeting Suggestions

- **Location:** Your service area + 25-mile radius
- **Age:** 30–65
- **Interests:** Home improvement, smart home, home theater, luxury homes,
  Control4, Crestron, Sonos, Lutron, high-end AV
- **Behaviors:** Recent homebuyers, engaged shoppers, luxury goods
- **Placements:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Instagram Reels, Stories

## Creative Direction

- **Video (best performer):** 15–30s walkthrough — dark room, voice command,
  lights dim, shades drop, screen lights up, music swells. End on logo + CTA.
- **Image option:** Dim, cinematic living room with backlit TV, in-ceiling
  speakers visible, tablet/phone showing the ELAN app in-hand.
- **Carousel option:** 1) Theater 2) Multi-room audio 3) Lighting scene
  4) App control 5) "Book free consult" card.
